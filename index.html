a little about me

Ideas first, then the plan that makes them real.

I'm happiest in the space between a big idea and the day it works. I like mapping the moving parts, finding the step everyone skipped, and keeping a team pointed the same way.

I studied software engineering, so I can talk to builders in their language — but my strength is strategy and operations: deciding what to build, for whom, and how it runs once it's live. Outside work I write about life, people and staying hopeful on my blog, As a Happaholic.
